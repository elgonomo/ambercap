WHO AM I

I’m Chris Lucas, also known as oGnomo or Ulterior Silence. I compose, record and design sound for games: from authentic chiptune made with old-school trackers to modern production.

Retro styles are where I’m most at home, and I love working within the limits of old hardware, where every channel counts. That approach carries into my modern music too, which is contemporary in sound but always inspired by the retro games I grew up with.

I trained as a composer and studio audio producer, and I build my own gear, from fuzz pedals to piezo boxes and many others. I’m currently building unique sound packs recorded from scratch with my piezo box/guitar hybrid (featured in Projects), running those raw sounds through my pedalboard and tape machine. Stay tuned if you’re interested.
